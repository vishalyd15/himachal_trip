Himachal Pradesh (9D/8N)

Day 1 15th Jan Friday (Mumbai → Chandigarh):
- 15th Jan 18:43 train from BDTS to Ambala Cant Jn (reach 19:05 16th Jan) — ~1,570 km, ~24 hrs

Day 2 16th Jan Saturday (Chandigarh → Manali):

- 16th jan Train (19:53) to Chandigarh (20:30) — Ambala → Chandigarh ~45–65 km by rail
- Chandigarh station → ISBT Sector 43 bus stand: ~10 km, ~25 min by cab/auto (check which stand your bus leaves from)
- HRTC Bus Last bus until 23:00, we'll catch 21:05 — Chandigarh → Manali ~310 km, ~10 hrs
- Reach Manali at 7 am (17th Jan):
- Hotel check-in

Day 3 (17th Jan Sunday) — Rest + Manali local:
- Freshen up, breakfast, rest till noon (ask the hotel for early check-in in advance)

Afternoon (12:30–5:00 PM) — Manali local (walk / local auto):
- 🛕 Hadimba Devi Temple + cedar forest (~2 km from Mall Road)
- ☕ Old Manali (cafés, Manu Temple) (~3 km from Mall Road)
- ♨️ Vashisht Temple + hot springs (~3 km from Mall Road)

Evening:
- Walk around Mall Road
- Dinner
- Early night (early start tomorrow)

🛏️ Overnight: Manali

Day 4 (18th Jan Monday) — Manali → Atal Tunnel → Sissu → Solang → Manali (~80 km round trip):
- Book a local Manali taxi with snow chains (outside taxis usually aren't allowed for Solang/Atal Tunnel)
- Rent snow boots/jackets in Manali or at Solang
- 6:00–6:30 AM — Breakfast
- 7:00 AM — Leave Manali (Sissu first; traffic towards the tunnel builds up by late morning)

Manali → Solang (~14 km) → Atal Tunnel South Portal (~12 km)
Stop 1: Atal Tunnel, ~9 km long (no stopping inside; photos at the North Portal)
Stop 2: Sissu, North Portal → Sissu ~6–10 km (~40 km / ~1.5–2 hrs from Manali in total)
Do:
- Sissu viewpoint
- Chandra River area
- Snow photography
- Tea/food
- Village exploration

~11:30 AM — Head back through Atal Tunnel to Solang (~26 km, ~1 hr)

Stop 3: Solang Valley (~12:30–3:30 PM)
Activities
🎿 Skiing
❄️ Snow activities
📸 Photography
🚡 Ropeway, if operating
🏔️ Mountain views

~4:00 PM — Leave Solang → Manali (~14 km, ~40 min), back before dark (roads get icy after sunset)

Plan B: if the road past Solang is closed after fresh snow, spend the day at Solang and add the Jogini Falls walk (from Vashisht).

🛏️ Overnight: Manali

Day 5 (19th Jan Tuesday) — Manali → Kullu → Kasol → Manikaran (~90 km):
- Check out. From today, use one multi-day cab (Manali → Kasol → Bir → Barot → Amritsar)
- 8:00 AM — Leave Manali: Manali → Kullu ~40 km, ~1.5 hrs
- 9:00–10:00 AM — Kullu (spend 2 hrs here)
Visit:
Raghunath Temple
Then:
- Kullu market
- Local handicrafts
- Kullu shawls/caps
- Beas River views

Afternoon — Kullu → Bhuntar (~10 km) → Kasol (~31 km), ~1.5 hrs; around 1–2 PM here
Check into your hotel, lunch.

2:30–4:00 PM — 🥾 Chalal trek (if willing):
- ~2–2.5 km each way on foot, ~30–45 min one way
- Starts from Kasol across the Parvati River bridge; easy walk along the river through pine forest
- Wear shoes with good grip, shaded parts of the trail can be icy in January

~4:15 PM — Kasol → Manikaran (~4 km, ~15 min), so you're there before dark
Drive to Manikaran Sahib

Visit:
- Gurudwara (langar)
- Hot springs
- Temple area
- Parvati River

~6:00 PM — Back to Kasol (~4 km), evening explore:
- Kasol market
- Parvati River
- Riverside cafés (January is off-season, some may be closed)
- Local shops

🛏️ Overnight: Kasol

Day 6 (20th Jan Wednesday) — Kasol → Bir + Paragliding (~155–200 km):
- 6:00 AM — Wake up, breakfast, pack
- 7:00 AM — Kasol → Bhuntar (~31 km) → Mandi (~55 km) → Jogindernagar (~55 km) → Bir (~16 km), ~5.5–6 hrs
- 12:30–1:00 PM — Arrive Bir, check in, lunch

2:00–3:00 PM — PARAGLIDING: Billing → Bir
- Bir → Billing take-off is ~14 km uphill by road (the operator drives you up), then you fly down to Bir
- Pre-book with a registered operator
- Weather-dependent in January; if cancelled, use tomorrow morning as backup

Evening:
- Tibetan Colony
- Bir market
- Cafés
- Rest and have fun

🛏️ Overnight: Bir

Day 7 (21st Jan Thursday) — Bir → Barot Valley 🌲🏔️ (~55–80 km, ~3 hrs):
- 8:00 AM — Breakfast
- 8:30–10:30 AM — Backup paragliding slot (if yesterday got cancelled), otherwise final Bir exploration:
  - Monastery
  - Tea gardens
- 11:00 AM — Leave Bir
Route: Bir → Jogindernagar (~16 km) → Ghatasani (~14 km) → Barot (~25 km)
(Shortcut: the newer Bir–Billing–Barot road is only ~38 km, but it climbs over the Dhauladhar and may be snowbound in January; ask in Bir whether it's open)
(The Ghatasani–Barot road can get snow in January; check the day before)
- ~2:00–3:00 PM — Reach Barot
Check in to a valley-view/riverside homestay and have lunch.

4:00–6:00 PM — Explore Barot
Don't try to turn Barot into a sightseeing checklist.
Do:
- 🌊 Uhl River
- 🏔️ Barot Valley viewpoints
- 🌲 Short walk around the valley
- 💧 Barot reservoir/hydroelectric area
- 📸 Photography
- ☕ Relax at your homestay/café

6:00 PM onwards — Relax (January gets dark early)
Dinner at the homestay.

🛏️ Overnight: Barot

Day 8 (22nd Jan Friday) — Barot → Amritsar 🛕🇮🇳 (~320–370 km, ~9–10 hrs):
- 8:00–9:00 AM — Breakfast
- Morning in Barot: walk along the Uhl River, morning photos, relax at the homestay
- 12:00 PM — Leave Barot (the Barot–Ghatasani stretch is best around midday, when any ice has melted)
Route: Barot → Ghatasani (~25 km) → Jogindernagar (~14 km) → Baijnath (~26 km) → Palampur (~17 km) → Kangra (~35 km) → Pathankot (~90 km) → Amritsar (~108 km)
- ~2:30 PM — Lunch on the way (Baijnath/Palampur)
- ~6:30–7:00 PM — Pathankot (dark by now; the plains stretch to Amritsar can get foggy at night, so let the driver take it slow)
- ~9:30–10:30 PM — Reach Amritsar, hotel check-in, dinner
- Golden Temple: tomorrow early morning (or a short late-night visit if you have the energy, it's open all night)

🛏️ Overnight: Amritsar

- Wagah Border ceremony (~30 km from Amritsar, ~45 min) — late afternoon (~4:15–4:30 PM in winter, reach ~1.5 hrs early for seats; Saturday = bigger crowd). Only if the train leaves after ~8 PM
