Day 3 (17th Jan Sunday) — Rest + Solang Valley + Manali local:
- Freshen up, breakfast, rest till ~10:30 AM (ask the hotel for early check-in in advance)
- Book a local Manali taxi for Solang (round trip)

11:45 AM–2:30 PM — Solang Valley (no police permission needed up to Solang)

3:15–5:15 PM — Manali local (walk / local auto):

Day 4 (18th Jan Monday) — Manali → Atal Tunnel → Sissu → Manali + Vashisht (~80 km round trip):

⚠️ Check first (evening of 17th + morning of 18th):
- Sissu tourism ban: every winter Sissu Panchayat shuts tourism for the Halda/Losar festivals
  (2024: 15 Jan–28 Feb; 2026: 20 Jan–28 Feb). 2027 dates not announced yet (as of Nov 2026);
  18th Jan is right at the edge. The notice usually comes out in early–mid January.
- Road beyond Solang: after fresh snow, police stop tourist vehicles at Solang Nala, sometimes for days
  (happened in Jan 2021, 2022, 2023 and 2026). Ask the hotel/taxi driver the evening before.

Taxi & paperwork:
- Book a local Manali 4x4 taxi (Scorpio/Thar etc.) with snow chains; after snow only 4x4s with chains are let beyond Solang
- Vehicles entering Lahaul through the tunnel may need e-Aagman registration (eaagman.hp.gov.in); local drivers usually handle it
- Small entry taxes (Sissu SADA tax ~₹200–300 per car, Manali green tax); check if included in the taxi fare
- Carry cash; wear the snow boots/jackets (rent in Manali the evening before)

- 8:30 AM — Breakfast
- 9:00 AM — Leave Manali → Solang Nala (~14 km, ~40 min)

~10:00 AM — Solang Nala police barrier (in past winters tourist vehicles were let beyond Solang only from ~10 AM
and had to come back through the tunnel by ~3–4 PM). Solang is already done on Day 3, so drive straight on.

Solang → Atal Tunnel South Portal (~12 km)
Atal Tunnel: ~9 km long, no toll, no permit (no stopping inside; follow police on where you can park after the tunnel, e.g. Sissu helipad area)
North Portal → Sissu ~6–10 km (~40 km / ~1.5–2 hrs from Manali in total)
Do:
- Sissu viewpoint
- Chandra River area
- Sissu waterfall (partly frozen in deep winter)
- Snow photography
- Tea/food at the small dhabas on the main road (may be shut if the tourism ban is on)
- Village exploration

By ~2:00–2:30 PM — Head back through Atal Tunnel (past winter rules: return from the tunnel by 3–4 PM)
~3:30–4:00 PM — Back in Manali (sunset ~5:40 PM; black ice after sunset)

4:00–5:30 PM — ♨️ Vashisht Temple + hot springs (~3 km from Mall Road), a warm end after the cold day

Weather: Manali ~−2°C to 10°C; Sissu ~−2°C to 5°C by day, −8°C to −15°C at night.
Dress in layers + gloves, cap, sunglasses (snow glare).

Scenarios:
- Road open + Sissu open → plan as above
- Road open but Sissu tourism ban on → ask police at Solang if you can go up to the North Portal for snow/photos; otherwise a second, relaxed Solang visit (whatever you skipped on Day 3)
- Road closed beyond Solang (fresh snow) → second Solang visit or rest morning, then Vashisht + Jogini Falls walk (from Vashisht village)
- Heavy snowfall that day → don't go beyond Solang even if allowed; tourists have been stranded near the tunnel in January snow

🛏️ Overnight: Manali

Day 5 (19th Jan Tuesday) — Manali → Kullu → Kasol → Manikaran (~90 km):

Afternoon — Kullu → Bhuntar (~10 km) → Kasol (~31 km), ~1.5 hrs; around 1–2 PM here

2:30–4:00 PM — 🥾 Chalal trek (if willing):

~4:15 PM — Kasol → Manikaran (~4 km, ~15 min), so you're there before dark
Drive to Manikaran Sahib

~6:00 PM — Back to Kasol (~4 km), evening explore:

🛏️ Overnight: Kasol

Day 6 (20th Jan Wednesday) — Kasol → Bir + Paragliding (~155–200 km):

TRANSPORT & BOOKINGS (group of 6, local transport)
(Timings/fares from booking sites and travel guides, Nov 2026. They often disagree, so recheck a few days before.)

📅 Booking calendar (trains and HRTC open 60 days ahead; IRCTC opens at 8 AM):
- 16 Nov 2026 (TODAY) → BDTS → Ambala Cantt train for 15 Jan
- 17 Nov 2026 → 12005 Kalka Shatabdi Ambala → Chandigarh (16 Jan) + HRTC bus Chandigarh → Manali (16 Jan)
- 24 Nov 2026 → Amritsar → Mumbai train (23 Jan)
- One IRCTC ticket (PNR) takes up to 6 passengers, so all 6 fit on one booking

Day 2 (16 Jan) Ambala → Chandigarh → Manali:
- Ambala Cantt → Chandigarh: 12005 Kalka Shatabdi, 19:53 → 20:30 (daily). Book chair car (CC) on IRCTC
- Chandigarh station → ISBT Sector 43: Uber/Ola XL or 2 autos, ~25 min
- ISBT 43 → Manali: HRTC HimSuta Volvo ~21:30 / 22:00 (reaches ~8 AM), ~₹900–1,500 per seat;
  ordinary HRTC buses until ~23:00. Book 6 seats on online.hrtchp.com (or redBus)
- Prefer the ~22:00 Volvo over 21:05: buffer if the Mumbai train runs late in the fog
- Backup: private Volvos also leave from opposite ISBT 43 late in the evening

Day 3 (17 Jan) Manali → Solang → Manali:
- Manali taxi union SUV (Innova/Scorpio, fits 6): ~₹2,000–4,500 per vehicle for Solang round trip
- Hadimba / Old Manali: walk or autos

Day 4 (18 Jan) Manali → Sissu → Manali:
- Manali taxi union 4x4 SUV (Scorpio) with snow chains: ~₹3,000–6,000 per vehicle; book the evening before
- The HRTC Manali → Keylong bus passes Sissu, but winter services are few and the return is unreliable → take the taxi

Day 5 (19 Jan) Manali → Kullu → Kasol → Manikaran:
- Manali → Kullu: frequent HRTC/private local buses from Manali bus stand (~1.5 hrs), no booking, pay on board
- Kullu → Bhuntar → Kasol: buses towards Manikaran from Kullu/Bhuntar (~1–1.5 hrs)
- Kasol ↔ Manikaran: the same Bhuntar–Manikaran local buses (run ~6 AM–6:30 PM) or a shared taxi
- Carry cash for bus tickets

Day 6 (20 Jan) Kasol → Bir:
- No direct daytime bus. Bus route: Kasol → Bhuntar (early bus) → HRTC bus towards Baijnath/Palampur/Dharamshala
  via Mandi–Jogindernagar → get off at the Bir turn-off → local taxi to Bir.
  ~6–7 hrs with changes, reaching ~1–2 PM: too tight for a 2 PM paragliding slot
- Recommended: one-way taxi for 6 (Innova Crysta / Ertiga), ~₹4,000–6,000 per vehicle (~₹700–1,000 each), leave 7 AM
- The paragliding operator picks you up in Bir and drives you up to Billing

Day 7 (21 Jan) Bir → Barot:
- Bus from Bir (Chowgan Chowk) towards Jogindernagar/Barot, or bus to Jogindernagar and change for Barot
- From Ghatasani: shared jeeps/buses to Barot every 45–60 min until ~5 PM (~₹50–60 each)
- Buses to/from Barot get scarce after ~4 PM in winter

Day 8 (22 Jan) Barot → Amritsar:
- Direct HRTC buses to Amritsar leave Baijnath/Palampur only in the morning (last ~11:15 AM / ~11:50 AM, ~7.5 hrs),
  so leaving Barot at 12 PM by bus doesn't work
- Recommended: one-way taxi Barot → Amritsar (Ertiga/Innova), ~₹4,000–6,000 per vehicle (~₹700–1,000 each)
- Bus option only if you leave Barot by ~7 AM: jeep/bus to Jogindernagar → HRTC bus to Amritsar (arrive ~6–7 PM)

Day 9 (23 Jan) Amritsar → Mumbai:
- Golden Temple, Jallianwala Bagh, Partition Museum: all walking distance
- Wagah: shared rickshaws from around the Golden Temple, ~₹150 each, leave ~2 PM in winter
- Trains to Mumbai:
  - 12904 Golden Temple Mail: 18:55 → Mumbai Central next day ~23:35 (~29 hrs)
  - 12926 Paschim Express: 07:20 → Bandra Terminus next day ~14:40 (~31 hrs)
- ⚠️ Wagah (back in town ~6:30–7 PM) clashes with the 18:55 train: either skip Wagah,
  or stay one more night and take Paschim Express at 07:20 on 24 Jan

Luggage: Innova Crysta / Ertiga seat 6 passengers but luggage space is tight; ask for a roof carrier or take a Tempo Traveller
