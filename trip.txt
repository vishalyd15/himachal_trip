Himachal Pradesh (9D/8N)

- Hotel check-in

Day 3 (17th Jan Sunday) — Rest + Solang Valley + Manali local:
- Freshen up, breakfast, rest till ~10:30 AM (ask the hotel for early check-in in advance)
- Book a local Manali taxi for Solang (round trip)

11:00 AM — Manali → Solang (~14 km, ~40 min)
11:45 AM–2:30 PM — Solang Valley (no police permission needed up to Solang)
Activities

2:30 PM — Solang → Manali (~14 km, ~40 min)

3:15–5:15 PM — Manali local (walk / local auto):

Day 4 (18th Jan Monday) — Manali → Atal Tunnel → Sissu → Manali + Vashisht (~80 km round trip):

⚠️ Check first (evening of 17th + morning of 18th):
- Sissu tourism ban: every winter Sissu Panchayat shuts tourism for the Halda/Losar festivals
  (2024: 15 Jan–28 Feb; 2026: 20 Jan–28 Feb). 2027 dates not announced yet (as of Nov 2026);
  18th Jan is right at the edge. The notice usually comes out in early–mid January.
- Road beyond Solang: after fresh snow, police stop tourist vehicles at Solang Nala, sometimes for days
  (happened in Jan 2021, 2022, 2023 and 2026). Ask the hotel/taxi driver the evening before.

Taxi & paperwork:
- Book a local Manali 4x4 taxi (Scorpio/Thar etc.) with snow chains; after snow only 4x4s with chains are let beyond Solang
- Vehicles entering Lahaul through the tunnel may need e-Aagman registration (eaagman.hp.gov.in); local drivers usually handle it
- Small entry taxes (Sissu SADA tax ~₹200–300 per car, Manali green tax); check if included in the taxi fare
- Carry cash; wear the snow boots/jackets (rent in Manali the evening before)

- 8:30 AM — Breakfast
- 9:00 AM — Leave Manali → Solang Nala (~14 km, ~40 min)

~10:00 AM — Solang Nala police barrier (in past winters tourist vehicles were let beyond Solang only from ~10 AM
and had to come back through the tunnel by ~3–4 PM). Solang is already done on Day 3, so drive straight on.

Solang → Atal Tunnel South Portal (~12 km)
Atal Tunnel: ~9 km long, no toll, no permit (no stopping inside; follow police on where you can park after the tunnel, e.g. Sissu helipad area)
North Portal → Sissu ~6–10 km (~40 km / ~1.5–2 hrs from Manali in total)
Do:
- Sissu viewpoint
- Chandra River area
- Sissu waterfall (partly frozen in deep winter)
- Snow photography
- Tea/food at the small dhabas on the main road (may be shut if the tourism ban is on)
- Village exploration

By ~2:00–2:30 PM — Head back through Atal Tunnel (past winter rules: return from the tunnel by 3–4 PM)
~3:30–4:00 PM — Back in Manali (sunset ~5:40 PM; black ice after sunset)

4:00–5:30 PM — ♨️ Vashisht Temple + hot springs (~3 km from Mall Road), a warm end after the cold day

Weather: Manali ~−2°C to 10°C; Sissu ~−2°C to 5°C by day, −8°C to −15°C at night.
Dress in layers + gloves, cap, sunglasses (snow glare).

Scenarios:
- Road open + Sissu open → plan as above
- Road open but Sissu tourism ban on → ask police at Solang if you can go up to the North Portal for snow/photos; otherwise a second, relaxed Solang visit (whatever you skipped on Day 3)
- Road closed beyond Solang (fresh snow) → second Solang visit or rest morning, then Vashisht + Jogini Falls walk (from Vashisht village)
- Heavy snowfall that day → don't go beyond Solang even if allowed; tourists have been stranded near the tunnel in January snow

🛏️ Overnight: Manali

Day 5 (19th Jan Tuesday) — Manali → Kullu → Kasol → Manikaran (~90 km):
- Check out. From today, use one multi-day cab (Manali → Kasol → Bir → Barot → Amritsar)
- 8:00 AM — Leave Manali: Manali → Kullu ~40 km, ~1.5 hrs
- 9:00–10:00 AM — Kullu (spend 2 hrs here)

Raghunath Temple
Then:

Afternoon — Kullu → Bhuntar (~10 km) → Kasol (~31 km), ~1.5 hrs; around 1–2 PM here
Check into your hotel, lunch.

2:30–4:00 PM — 🥾 Chalal trek (if willing):
- ~2–2.5 km each way on foot, ~30–45 min one way
- Starts from Kasol across the Parvati River bridge; easy walk along the river through pine forest
- Wear shoes with good grip, shaded parts of the trail can be icy in January

~4:15 PM — Kasol → Manikaran (~4 km, ~15 min), so you're there before dark
Drive to Manikaran Sahib

Visit:
- Gurudwara (langar)
- Hot springs
- Temple area
- Parvati River

~6:00 PM — Back to Kasol (~4 km), evening explore:
- Kasol market
- Parvati River
- Riverside cafés (January is off-season, some may be closed)
- Local shops

🛏️ Overnight: Kasol

Day 6 (20th Jan Wednesday) — Kasol → Bir + Paragliding (~155–200 km):
