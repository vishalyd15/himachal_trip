Himachal Pradesh (9D/8N)

Day 1 22nd Jan Friday (Mumbai → Chandigarh):
- 22nd Jan 18:43 train from BDTS to Ambala Cant Jn (reach 19:05 23rd Jan) — ~1,570 km, ~24 hrs

Day 2 23rd Jan Saturday (Chandigarh → Manali):

- 23rd jan Train (19:53) to Chandigarh (20:30) — Ambala → Chandigarh ~45–65 km by rail
- Chandigarh station → ISBT Sector 43 bus stand: ~10 km, ~25 min by cab/auto (check which stand your bus leaves from)
- HRTC Bus Last bus until 23:00, we'll catch 21:05 — Chandigarh → Manali ~310 km, ~10 hrs

- Hotel check-in

Day 3 (24th Jan Sunday) — Rest + Solang Valley + Manali local:
- Freshen up, breakfast, rest till ~10:30 AM (ask the hotel for early check-in in advance)
- Book a local Manali taxi for Solang (round trip)

11:00 AM — Manali → Solang (~14 km, ~40 min)
11:45 AM–2:30 PM — Solang Valley (no police permission needed up to Solang)
Activities

❄️ Snow activities (snow scooter, tube slide)
📸 Photography
🚡 Ropeway, if operating (~9 AM–5 PM, ~₹500–750 per person)
🏔️ Mountain views

- Lunch at Solang

2:30 PM — Solang → Manali (~14 km, ~40 min)

3:15–5:15 PM — Manali local (walk / local auto):
- 🛕 Hadimba Devi Temple + cedar forest (~2 km from Mall Road)
- ☕ Old Manali (cafés, Manu Temple) (~3 km from Mall Road, close to Hadimba)

Evening:

- Dinner

🛏️ Overnight: Manali

Day 4 (25th Jan Monday) — Manali → Atal Tunnel → Sissu → Manali + Vashisht (~80 km round trip):

⚠️ Check first (evening of 24th + morning of 25th):
- Sissu tourism ban: every winter Sissu Panchayat shuts tourism for the Halda/Losar festivals
  (2024: 15 Jan–28 Feb; 2026: 20 Jan–28 Feb). 2027 dates not announced yet (as of Nov 2026);
  25th Jan falls INSIDE the closure window of every recent year, so expect Sissu to be closed.
  The notice usually comes out in early–mid January.
- Road beyond Solang: after fresh snow, police stop tourist vehicles at Solang Nala, sometimes for days
  (happened in Jan 2021, 2022, 2023 and 2026). Ask the hotel/taxi driver the evening before.

Taxi & paperwork:
- Book a local Manali 4x4 taxi (Scorpio/Thar etc.) with snow chains; after snow only 4x4s with chains are let beyond Solang
- Vehicles entering Lahaul through the tunnel may need e-Aagman registration (eaagman.hp.gov.in); local drivers usually handle it
- Small entry taxes (Sissu SADA tax ~₹200–300 per car, Manali green tax); check if included in the taxi fare
- Carry cash; wear the snow boots/jackets (rent in Manali the evening before)

- 8:30 AM — Breakfast
- 9:00 AM — Leave Manali → Solang Nala (~14 km, ~40 min)

~10:00 AM — Solang Nala police barrier (in past winters tourist vehicles were let beyond Solang only from ~10 AM
and had to come back through the tunnel by ~3–4 PM). Solang is already done on Day 3, so drive straight on.

Solang → Atal Tunnel South Portal (~12 km)
Atal Tunnel: ~9 km long, no toll, no permit (no stopping inside; follow police on where you can park after the tunnel, e.g. Sissu helipad area)
North Portal → Sissu ~6–10 km (~40 km / ~1.5–2 hrs from Manali in total)
Do:
- Sissu viewpoint
- Chandra River area
- Sissu waterfall (partly frozen in deep winter)
- Snow photography
- Tea/food at the small dhabas on the main road (may be shut if the tourism ban is on)
- Village exploration

By ~2:00–2:30 PM — Head back through Atal Tunnel (past winter rules: return from the tunnel by 3–4 PM)
~3:30–4:00 PM — Back in Manali (sunset ~5:40 PM; black ice after sunset)

4:00–5:30 PM — ♨️ Vashisht Temple + hot springs (~3 km from Mall Road), a warm end after the cold day

Weather: Manali ~−2°C to 10°C; Sissu ~−2°C to 5°C by day, −8°C to −15°C at night.
Dress in layers + gloves, cap, sunglasses (snow glare).

Scenarios:
- Road open + Sissu open → plan as above
- Road open but Sissu tourism ban on → ask police at Solang if you can go up to the North Portal for snow/photos; otherwise a second, relaxed Solang visit (whatever you skipped on Day 3)
- Road closed beyond Solang (fresh snow) → second Solang visit or rest morning, then Vashisht + Jogini Falls walk (from Vashisht village)
- Heavy snowfall that day → don't go beyond Solang even if allowed; tourists have been stranded near the tunnel in January snow

🛏️ Overnight: Manali

Day 5 (26th Jan Tuesday) — Manali → Kullu → Kasol → Manikaran (~90 km):
- Check out. Local buses today (see TRANSPORT & BOOKINGS at the end)
- 8:00 AM — Leave Manali: Manali → Kullu ~40 km, ~1.5 hrs
- 9:00–10:00 AM — Kullu (spend 2 hrs here)
Visit:
Raghunath Temple
Then:
- Kullu market
- Local handicrafts
- Kullu shawls/caps
- Beas River views

Optional — Check rafting at Pirdi (~3–4 km past Kullu, on the way to Bhuntar)
- Beas rafting season is normally Mar–Jun & Sep–Nov; in late Jan it's most likely shut (freezing water)
- Ask at the rafting counters on the day; if anyone is running and it's licensed, it's a ~1 hr add-on
- Don't plan around it

Afternoon — Kullu → Bhuntar (~10 km) → Kasol (~31 km), ~1.5 hrs; around 1–2 PM here
Check into your hotel, lunch.

2:30–4:00 PM — 🥾 Chalal trek (if willing):
- ~2–2.5 km each way on foot, ~30–45 min one way
- Starts from Kasol across the Parvati River bridge; easy walk along the river through pine forest
- Wear shoes with good grip, shaded parts of the trail can be icy in January

~4:15 PM — Kasol → Manikaran (~4 km, ~15 min), so you're there before dark
Drive to Manikaran Sahib

Visit:
- Gurudwara (langar)
- Hot springs
- Temple area
- Parvati River

~6:00 PM — Back to Kasol (~4 km), evening explore:
- Kasol market
- Parvati River
- Riverside cafés (January is off-season, some may be closed)
- Local shops

🛏️ Overnight: Kasol

Day 6 (27th Jan Wednesday) — Kasol → Bir + Paragliding (~155–200 km):
- 6:30 AM — Wake up, breakfast, pack
- 7:30–8:00 AM — Kasol → Bhuntar (~31 km) → Mandi (~55 km) → Jogindernagar (~55 km) → Bir (~16 km), ~5.5–6 hrs
- ~1:30–2:00 PM — Arrive Bir, check in, quick lunch

~2:30–3:30 PM — PARAGLIDING: Billing → Bir
- Bir → Billing take-off is ~14 km uphill by road (the operator drives you up), then you fly down to Bir
- Pre-book with a registered operator: ask for a ~2:30–3 PM slot (a 2 PM slot is too tight now) and confirm the last flight time (dark early in January)
- Weather-dependent in January; if cancelled, use tomorrow morning as backup

Evening:
- Tibetan Colony
- Bir market
- Cafés
- Rest and have fun

🛏️ Overnight: Bir

Day 7 (28th Jan Thursday) — Bir → McLeodganj (Dharamshala) 🏔️ (~65 km, ~2–2.5 hrs):
- 8:30 AM — Relaxed breakfast
- Morning depends on yesterday's flight:
  - Paragliding slipped to today → fly this morning, then leave Bir at 12:00 PM
  - Paragliding done on Day 6 → slow Bir morning (monastery, tea gardens, cafés) and decide the leaving time on the day
    (leave ~10:30 if you want a stop at Palampur tea gardens or Norbulingka Institute on the way; otherwise leave at 12)
- 12:00 PM (latest) — Leave Bir by taxi
Route: Bir → Baijnath (~12–15 km) → Palampur (~17 km) → Dharamshala (~35 km)

- ~2:30 PM — HPCA Cricket Stadium, lower Dharamshala (on the way up): quick photo stop, snow peaks behind the pavilion
- ~3:15 PM — McLeodganj (~10 km up from lower Dharamshala): check in, late lunch (momos, thukpa, thenthuk, laphing)

4:15–5:15 PM — McLeodganj:
- 🛕 Tsuglagkhang (the Dalai Lama's temple complex): prayer wheels, valley views
- 🛍️ McLeodganj market: Tibetan stalls, prayer flags, singing bowls, woollens

~5:30 PM — 🌄 Naddi viewpoint for sunset over the Dhauladhar (~4 km, short taxi; optional)

Evening — cafés + dinner in McLeodganj
Weather: Dharamshala/McLeodganj ~2°C to 15°C, much milder than Manali/Sissu.

🛏️ Overnight: McLeodganj

Day 8 (29th Jan Friday) — McLeodganj → lower Dharamshala → Kangra Fort → Amritsar 🛕🇮🇳 (~200–220 km, ~5–6 hrs driving):
- 8:00 AM — Breakfast
- 8:30–10:30 AM — 💧 Bhagsunag temple + waterfall (~2 km walk from McLeodganj; the fall may be partly frozen), then coffee in ☕ Dharamkot (café village just above)

  (Ropeway shut → drive down instead, stopping at ⛪ St John in the Wilderness church on the way)

- ~1:00 PM — Leave for Amritsar
Route: Dharamshala → Kangra (~20 km) → Pathankot (~90 km) → Amritsar (~108 km)
- ~1:45–2:45 PM — 🏰 Kangra Fort (on the way)
- ~5:00–5:30 PM — Pathankot (dark soon; the plains stretch to Amritsar can get foggy at night, so let the driver take it slow)
- ~8:00–9:00 PM — Reach Amritsar, hotel check-in, dinner
- Golden Temple: tomorrow early morning (or a short late-night visit if you have the energy, it's open all night)

🛏️ Overnight: Amritsar

TRANSPORT & BOOKINGS (group of 6, local transport)
(Timings/fares from booking sites and travel guides, Nov 2026. They often disagree, so recheck a few days before.)

📅 Booking calendar (trains and HRTC open 60 days ahead; IRCTC opens at 8 AM):
- 23 Nov 2026 → BDTS → Ambala Cantt train for 22 Jan
- 24 Nov 2026 → 12005 Kalka Shatabdi Ambala → Chandigarh (23 Jan) + HRTC bus Chandigarh → Manali (23 Jan)
- 1 Dec 2026 → 12904 Golden Temple Mail Amritsar → Mumbai Central (30 Jan, 18:55)
- One IRCTC ticket (PNR) takes up to 6 passengers, so all 6 fit on one booking

Day 2 (23 Jan) Ambala → Chandigarh → Manali:
- Ambala Cantt → Chandigarh: 12005 Kalka Shatabdi, 19:53 → 20:30 (daily). Book chair car (CC) on IRCTC
- Chandigarh station → ISBT Sector 43: Uber/Ola XL or 2 autos, ~25 min
- ISBT 43 → Manali: HRTC HimSuta Volvo ~21:30 / 22:00 (reaches ~8 AM), ~₹900–1,500 per seat;
  ordinary HRTC buses until ~23:00. Book 6 seats on online.hrtchp.com (or redBus)
- Prefer the ~22:00 Volvo over 21:05: buffer if the Mumbai train runs late in the fog
- Backup: private Volvos also leave from opposite ISBT 43 late in the evening

Day 3 (24 Jan) Manali → Solang → Manali:
- Manali taxi union SUV (Innova/Scorpio, fits 6): ~₹2,000–4,500 per vehicle for Solang round trip
- Hadimba / Old Manali: walk or autos

Day 4 (25 Jan) Manali → Sissu → Manali:
- Manali taxi union 4x4 SUV (Scorpio) with snow chains: ~₹3,000–6,000 per vehicle; book the evening before
- The HRTC Manali → Keylong bus passes Sissu, but winter services are few and the return is unreliable → take the taxi

Day 5 (26 Jan) Manali → Kullu → Kasol → Manikaran:
- Manali → Kullu: frequent HRTC/private local buses from Manali bus stand (~1.5 hrs), no booking, pay on board
- Kullu → Bhuntar → Kasol: buses towards Manikaran from Kullu/Bhuntar (~1–1.5 hrs)
- Kasol ↔ Manikaran: the same Bhuntar–Manikaran local buses (run ~6 AM–6:30 PM) or a shared taxi
- Carry cash for bus tickets

Day 6 (27 Jan) Kasol → Bir:
- No direct daytime bus. Bus route: Kasol → Bhuntar (early bus) → HRTC bus towards Baijnath/Palampur/Dharamshala
  via Mandi–Jogindernagar → get off at the Bir turn-off → local taxi to Bir.
  ~6–7 hrs with changes, reaching mid-afternoon: too late for an afternoon paragliding slot
- Recommended: one-way taxi for 6 (Innova Crysta / Ertiga), ~₹4,000–6,000 per vehicle (~₹700–1,000 each), leave 7:30–8 AM
- The paragliding operator picks you up in Bir and drives you up to Billing

Day 7 (28 Jan) Bir → McLeodganj:
- Recommended: one-way taxi for 6 from Bir (short ~65 km run; get a quote in Bir). It allows the stadium stop and
  drops us at the McLeodganj hotel with the luggage
- Bus option: HRTC bus Bir/Baijnath → Dharamshala via Palampur (~2–3 hrs), then local bus/taxi up to McLeodganj (~10 km)

Day 8 (29 Jan) McLeodganj → Amritsar:
- Recommended: one-way taxi McLeodganj → Amritsar (Ertiga/Innova) with the ropeway + Kangra Fort stops; get 2–3
  quotes in McLeodganj on Day 7 evening (~200–220 km one way)
- Bus option: HRTC buses Dharamshala → Amritsar (~5–6 hrs) from the Dharamshala bus stand; booking sites disagree on
  timings, so check online.hrtchp.com / the counter. No Kangra Fort stop by bus
- Dharamshala Skyway: buy tickets at the lower/upper station; check it's running that morning (weather)

Day 9 (30 Jan) Amritsar → Mumbai:
- Golden Temple, Jallianwala Bagh, Partition Museum: all walking distance
- Train home: 12904 Golden Temple Mail, 18:55 → Mumbai Central next day ~23:35 (~29 hrs)
- Not doing the Wagah ceremony (it ends too late for this train)

Luggage: Innova Crysta / Ertiga seat 6 passengers but luggage space is tight; ask for a roof carrier or take a Tempo Traveller
